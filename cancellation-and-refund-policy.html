Cancellation, Refund & Strike Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026

This policy sets out what happens when an event is cancelled or missed, who gets refunded, and how strikes work. It applies to every Host, Organisation and attendee and forms part of the Terms of Service. Times are measured against the event's published start time in IST.

1. Key terms

Platform fee
The convenience fee Baethak charges attendees on a paid booking, plus GST.
Monetary penalty
An amount deducted from the Host's earnings or future payouts. By default it equals the platform fees and GST refunded to attendees for that event; Baethak may set a higher amount for Organisations or repeat cases, shown in the app before you host.
Approval-required event
An event where the Host must accept your join request before your booking is confirmed.
Request expiry
The automatic expiry of a paid join request that the Host has not accepted within 4 hours of payment.
Host No-Show
The Host cancels a paid event within 24 hours of the start, fails to check in within 500 m of the venue by 30 minutes before the start, or does not conduct the event.
Attendee no-show
A confirmed attendee who does not check in by QR code and does not attend the event.
Partial ban
A temporary block on hosting (for hosts) or joining (for attendees). Other features stay available. It lasts 7 days the first time and 14 days for any later partial ban, unless Baethak decides otherwise for serious cases.

2. Strike levels

Any strike on a host also removes extended hosting privileges (wider radius, higher limits) until the host completes 2 successful events again.

Strike expiry: if you have no new violation for 60 days, all your strikes reset to zero. Example: strike on 1 January, nothing further, reset on 2 March.

3. Paid events: cancelled or deleted by an individual host

If a Host cancels or deletes a paid event, for any reason, every attendee receives a full refund including the platform fee and GST. Deleting an event is treated as cancelling it. A cancellation 24 hours or more before the start is not a No-Show.

4. Paid events: Host No-Show

If a Host has not checked in by 1 hour before the start, Baethak sends reminders. If they have still not checked in by 30 minutes before the start, the event is cancelled automatically as a Host No-Show and every attendee is refunded in full, including the platform fee.

5. Paid events: cancelled by an Organisation

Every cancellation or deletion of a paid Organisation event is reviewed manually by Baethak, however far in advance it is made.

  • Attendees always receive a full refund including the platform fee.
  • A monetary penalty applies regardless of timing.
  • If Baethak finds the reason unjustified, the Organisation profile is suspended until further notice.
  • Organisation No-Shows also receive the No-Show strikes in Section 4.
  • The low-attendance window in Section 7 and accepted force majeure (Section 9) are the only exceptions to the penalty.

7. Low attendance

  1. Six hours before the start, Baethak checks how many tickets are booked. If fewer than 30% of the event's capacity is booked, the Host is notified and may choose to continue or cancel.
  2. The Host has 1 hour to decide. If the Host cancels in that window, every attendee gets a full refund including the platform fee, and the Host receives no penalty and no strike.
  3. If the Host does not respond within the hour, the event continues as confirmed.
  4. After that window closes, the Host cannot cancel the event under the low-attendance rule. Any later cancellation is a Host No-Show.
  5. If 30% or more of capacity is booked, the event proceeds automatically and no low-attendance option is offered.
  6. If an event has zero attendees, it is cancelled automatically with no penalty.

8. Attendees: refunds, cancellations and no-shows

8.1 Full refund (100%, including platform fee and GST)

You receive a full refund in these cases:

  1. Host rejection. The Host declines or rejects your paid join request.
  2. Request expiry. You have completed payment and the Host does not accept your request within 4 hours. The request expires automatically.
  3. Host cancels or deletes the event. The Host cancels or deletes the entire event, for any reason (see Sections 3 to 5, 7 and 9).
  4. Host removes you. The Host removes you from the guest list before the event starts.
  5. You cancel before approval. You have completed payment but cancel your request before the Host approves it.
  6. Event full or technical error. The event becomes full, or a ratio mismatch or technical error occurs, after successful payment. The full amount is queued automatically for refund.
  7. Material change. The Host changes the date, moves the start time by more than 1 hour, or reduces what is included, and you cancel because of it, whenever you cancel (see Host Agreement, Section 6.2). Under review

None of the cases in 8.1(1) to 8.1(6) gives you a strike.

8.2 Approval-required events

For an approval-required event, payment is collected when you send the request. Your booking is confirmed only when the app shows it as confirmed. Until then, 8.1(1), (2) and (5) apply.

8.3 Cancelling a confirmed booking

Except under 8.1(7), if you cancel a booking after the Host has approved it, for any reason and at any time, no refund is given. The full amount paid, including the platform fee and GST, is non-refundable. A booking you cancel before the event is not an attendee no-show and does not earn a strike. Under review

8.4 No-show

If you do not physically attend the event, no refund is given. A paid ticket that is not used is not refunded. A refusal of entry for intoxication, unsafe conduct or breach of the Attendee Terms is also not refunded.

9. Force majeure and safety cancellations

If an event cannot go ahead because of events outside the Host's reasonable control, such as government orders, severe weather warnings, natural disasters, civil unrest, a venue closure ordered by authorities, or a documented medical emergency, the Host must cancel promptly and report the reason. Attendees receive a full refund including the platform fee. If Baethak accepts the reason after review, no penalty or strike is applied.

Baethak may itself cancel any event for safety, legal or policy reasons, with a full refund to attendees.

10. How refunds are paid

Refunds are triggered automatically for: Host rejection, request expiry, host cancellation or deletion, Host No-Show, low-attendance cancellation, zero-attendee cancellation, removal of a guest by the Host, cancellation before approval, and event-full or technical-error cases.

  • Refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay, within 7 to 10 working days of the refund being approved or queued, depending on your bank.
  • Refunds are made only to the person who paid, in Indian Rupees.
  • Refunds to attendees for host-fault cancellations are recovered from the Host's event proceeds or future payouts. Refunds for rejection, request expiry, removal and event-full cases are made from the amount Baethak is holding for the booking, with no deduction from the Host's other earnings.

11. Disputes and appeals

If you believe a strike, penalty or refund decision is wrong, appeal within 7 days through Help > Email support@baethak.com with any evidence (for example screenshots, location records or messages). Baethak reviews appeals manually and may reverse strikes or penalties. Under review: confirm support email
