Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Baethak Technologies Private Limited ("Baethak", "we") collects when you use the Baethak app and website, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights you have. Baethak is the "Data Fiduciary" for your data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") and the DPDP Rules, 2025. We also follow the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules under it.

Baethak is only for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn that an account belongs to a minor, we will delete it.

3. Legal basis and consent

3.1 We process your data on the basis of your consent, which you give when you sign up and at specific steps. Face verification, precise location and background location each ask for separate consent at the point of use. Under review

3.2 We also process data without consent where the DPDP Act allows it for "legitimate uses", including where you have voluntarily provided data for a specific purpose, to comply with law or court orders, and in medical emergencies.

3.3 Withdrawing consent. You may withdraw consent at any time in Settings or by writing to support@baethak.com. Withdrawal is as easy as giving consent. Some features need certain data: for example, without face verification you cannot host or join events, and without location you cannot see nearby events or check in. Withdrawal does not affect processing already done.

4. What other users can see

  • Your profile card: name, photos, verified badge, ratings, hosting identity, communities you created, and organisations you own.
  • Your wall: public, or visible only to connections if you set it to private. Links to your social and music profiles (such as Instagram and Spotify) are shown only to your connections.
  • When you join an event: the host and other attendees can see that you are attending. Attendee lists may appear on the event card.
  • Posts tagged to an event are shown to that event's attendees and your connections. Other attendees are "soft-linked", not auto-tagged.
  • Organisation pages are always public, including their co-owners' names and profile links.
  • Event addresses are shown only to people who have joined; others see an approximate area.
  • Your live location is never shown to other users.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only as follows:

  • Hosts. When you join an event, its host receives your name, profile, ticket details and check-in status, so they can run the event. Hosts may use this only for that event.
  • Service providers (Data Processors) under written contracts: cloud hosting, Razorpay (payments), our face-verification provider (AWS Rekognition), our Aadhaar verification provider (organisation creators only), SMS/OTP and push-notification services, maps, analytics, crash reporting and customer support tools.
  • Law enforcement and government when required by law, court order or a lawful request under the IT Act, including to identify the originator of unlawful content or to assist an investigation.
  • Safety. To protect someone's life or safety, or to prevent fraud.
  • Business transfers. In a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

6. Where data is stored

Your data is stored on secure cloud servers in India and may be processed in other countries by our service providers, except countries the Government of India restricts. We protect transferred data under the same standards as this policy.

8. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of your personal data and how it is processed, and the identities of those it has been shared with
  • Correct, complete or update inaccurate data (your date of birth can only be corrected with proof, as it is locked for safety)
  • Erase your data, subject to legal retention
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Nominate a person to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated
  • Grievance redressal from us, and then from the Data Protection Board of India if you are not satisfied

Use Settings > Help, or email support@baethak.com. We may verify your identity before acting. We respond within the time the DPDP Rules require, and in any case within 30 days.

14. Reporting inappropriate content and user misconduct

Users can report any inappropriate, offensive, abusive, or otherwise prohibited content posted on Baethak. Once a report is received, Baethak will review the complaint within 24 hours.

If the complaint is found to be valid, Baethak reserves the right to take appropriate action against the reported user, including removing the content, issuing a warning, restricting account access, suspending the account, or permanently terminating the account, depending on the severity of the violation.

Baethak may also take immediate action in cases involving serious safety concerns, harassment, threats, or other violations of applicable laws and platform policies.

15. Suspension and termination

Baethak reserves the right to suspend or permanently terminate any user account found to be involved in illegal activities, harassment, or other serious misconduct. Baethak will cooperate with law enforcement authorities and provide relevant information or evidence as required by applicable laws.
